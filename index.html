      How to play

      Controls

      Swipe anywhere on a touch screen. On a keyboard, use the arrow keys, WASD or HJKL, and Esc to pause. A gamepad works with the D-pad or left stick.

      You can queue up to three turns ahead, so quick double turns land.

      Scoring

      A fruit is worth 10. Eat the next one within 3.5 seconds to raise the multiplier, up to ×5. The bar under the board shows how long you have.

      Gold stars appear now and then for 50 and vanish when their ring runs out.

      Walls and gates

      A solid edge is a wall. A dashed stretch in the fruit colour is a gate: go through it and you come back in on the opposite edge, in the same row or column. The short posts mark where a gate ends.

      Classic has no gates. Blitz and Zen are all gates. Gates and Daily mix the two and add walls inside the board.

      Gates mode

      Each room asks for a few fruit. Eat them and the room is cleared: you bank a bonus of 100 times the room number, and a new room is built with fewer gates and more walls. Your snake starts short again in every room.

      Replays

      Every game can be replayed. Share replay copies a link that plays the whole run, move for move, for anyone who opens it.
